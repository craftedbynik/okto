# okto — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-21_

okto is a Chrome extension that lets you select multiple passages of text on a web page and copy them to your clipboard at once.

## Data collection

okto collects **no data**. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and makes no network requests of any kind.

## What the extension does with page content

- When you hold Cmd/Ctrl and select text, okto keeps track of those selections **in memory, inside the page you are viewing**, so it can highlight them and copy them together.
- When you press Cmd/Ctrl+C, the selected text is written to **your system clipboard** — exactly as a normal copy would do.
- Nothing is stored after you leave the page, and nothing ever leaves your device.

## Permissions

- **clipboardWrite** — needed to place your combined selections on the clipboard when you press Cmd/Ctrl+C.
- **Access to all websites** (content script) — multi-select has to work on whatever page you are reading. The script is passive until you hold the modifier key; on pages where you never do, it adds nothing to the page and does nothing.

## Third parties

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to anyone. There are no third-party services involved.
